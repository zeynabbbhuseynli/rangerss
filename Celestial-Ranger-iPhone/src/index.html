Your mission

To scan the stars, safeguard the cosmos, and ensure no explorer is ever left behind. As Celestial Rangers at Epic Universe, our highest duty is to locate, guide, and protect lost star travelers, reuniting them with their home constellations using the power of galactic teamwork.
