# TestFlight metadata draft

App name: Celestial Ranger
Version: 0.2.0
Subtitle: A botanical story adventure

## Beta description

Join Fernan and Oakley in a fictional botanical scavenger hunt. Take the Ranger Oath, explore five discoveries, solve trivia, collect stamps, write field notes, and complete your constellation. Ask the characters about clues and your progress with typed messages or speech input.

This is an unofficial fan-made concept prototype. Park navigation, location, distances, snack selections and orders are simulated. Character conversations use authored story replies in this beta; no live AI service is connected.

## What to test

- Complete all three onboarding steps and switch Ranger modes.
- Follow all five missions, answer trivia, collect stamps and view the certificate.
- Close and reopen the app; verify saved stamps and field notes.
- Ask Oakley and Fernan for a clue and your next mission.
- Grant or deny microphone and speech permissions; verify typing still works.
- Turn on read-aloud, stop playback, and switch characters.
- Share the completion certificate using the native share sheet.

## Review notes

No account or login is needed. Start a new adventure, take the oath, choose a mode and begin. Testers can tap any booth and collect its stamp; physical park presence is not required. Collect all five to unlock the recap. Native capabilities include user-initiated speech recognition, text-to-speech, haptic feedback, local preferences, and the share sheet. AI controls are hidden in the default build.
